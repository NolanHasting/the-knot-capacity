FAQ and gym hours

Knot Capacity is an independent project for The Knot Climbing Gym in Gainesville. The gym’s public counter rises with check-ins, and staff periodically correct it. A drop can suggest a recount. Forecasts use past readings and can change throughout the day.

Regular hours are 7 AM–11 PM on weekdays and 7 AM–10 PM on weekends, Eastern time. Check the gym’s closure notices before visiting.
