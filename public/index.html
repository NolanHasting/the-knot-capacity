About The Knot crowd tracker

When is The Knot open?

Regular hours in Gainesville: Monday–Friday 7 AM–11 PM; Saturday–Sunday 7 AM–10 PM Eastern. Check official closures and changes before visiting.

Why can the live count be higher than the actual crowd?

The reported count rises with check-ins and is periodically corrected by staff. Downward changes are inferred recounts, not independently confirmed headcounts. The adjusted estimate accounts for possible departures when a recount adjustment is available; otherwise it uses the reported count.

What is the best time to climb?

Find times that suit your preferred crowd level and schedule, or check the weekly heatmap for typical crowds. Forecasts are not guarantees.

Install the tracker on your phone or Mac.

Check how busy The Knot Climbing Gym in Gainesville is before heading over. The live count comes from the gym's public occupancy reading. Downward corrections may indicate staff recounts. Forecasts use previous observations. Predictions can change as new data arrives.
